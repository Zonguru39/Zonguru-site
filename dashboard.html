About Us

The Zonguru platform helps merchants who sell through Amazon improve product visibility, increase sales, and build stronger online businesses.

Our Mission

We want merchants to make better product decisions with clearer information, practical tools, and a simple workflow. From discovering opportunities to improving product performance, Zonguru is built around one goal: helping sellers work smarter and grow sustainably.

What We Do

Zonguru brings product-focused tools and services together in one platform. We help merchants organize product activities, understand performance, manage orders, and keep track of important account activity in one convenient place.

Our approach is designed to make e-commerce operations easier to understand and easier to manage. We believe useful technology should reduce complexity, give sellers better visibility, and help them spend more time on the decisions that matter.

Seller Focus

Tools designed around practical merchant workflows.

Data & Performance

Clear information to help merchants measure progress.

Simple Experience

A clean platform that keeps everyday tasks easy to follow.

Continuous Improvement

We keep improving the experience as merchant needs evolve.

Why Zonguru?

Whether you are building a new product business or growing an established store, Zonguru is designed to give you a more organized, informed, and efficient way to manage your e-commerce journey.

Zonguru is an independent platform. References to Amazon describe the marketplace ecosystem in which merchants operate and do not imply that this platform is owned or operated by Amazon.
